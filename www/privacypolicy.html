Training Log - Privacy

Training Log is a personal workout tracker. Everything it stores stays on this phone.

Health data

If you connect Samsung Health through Health Connect, the app reads heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, breathing rate, steps, distance, calories, sleep, weight, body fat, VO2 max, blood pressure and recorded workouts. It only reads; it never writes to or changes your health data.

This data is used only to show your health numbers beside your workouts, on charts, and in PDF reports you choose to create. It is not uploaded, sold or shared with anyone. A PDF report or backup leaves the phone only if you send it yourself.

Turning it off

Tap More → Samsung Health → Disconnect, or remove the app's access in Health Connect settings at any time.
